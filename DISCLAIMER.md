# Clinical Safety Disclaimer

This repository is an exploratory Clinical AI safety portfolio project.

It is intended for:

- research
- education
- experimentation
- AI evaluation
- portfolio demonstration

It is **not a medical device** and has not undergone regulatory or formal clinical validation.

The Clinical AI Response Auditor must not be used to:

- diagnose patients
- provide medical treatment
- replace professional clinical judgement
- make autonomous clinical decisions
- determine emergency care for real patients

The clinical scenarios included in this repository are synthetic evaluation cases created for AI safety testing.

Performance results are based on a small experimental validation dataset and should not be interpreted as evidence of real-world clinical effectiveness.

Any future clinical deployment would require substantially larger validation datasets, independent clinician review, evidence-grounded evaluation, prospective testing and appropriate regulatory assessment.
